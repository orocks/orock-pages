Solar-Powered ESP32 Weather Station via LoRa

Research compilation: curated resources and a BOM for a terrace-mounted ESP32 weather station communicating via LoRa to a local gateway.

  • ESP32 Deep Sleep: Ultra-Low Power IoT Sensor Node Build — Zbotic (2026). Wake sources, RTC memory, sensor wiring, firmware, and real battery life estimation.

Estimated Power Budget

ModeCurrentDuration/daymAh/day
Deep sleep~6µA (C3) / ~20µA (WROOM)23h 55m~0.5
Sensor read (BME280 + VEML6075 I2C)~0.5mA5s~0.0
LoRa TX (20dBm, 10s)~120mA10s/cycle~0.3
MCU init/wake~50mA2s~0.0
Total per cycle~0.8 mAh

With a 3500 mAh battery: ~4,300 cycles at 1 cycle/hour = ~6 months autonomy. Add 30-50% margin for aging, cold, and cloudy days = 4-6 months real-world.
